Why this framing earns trust

A Power Apps pitch that skips licensing, Dataverse capacity, and the state-machine/RBAC/audit mismatch will be shredded by the first enterprise reviewer. The doc flips it: name the hard parts of this app that Power Apps is worst at (the 6-status guarded machine with per-transition timestamps, 6-role default-deny, immutable auditLogs, indexed parts search, invoice approve/pay/balance) — then price the rebuild honestly vs finishing the shop. For Excel/PDF, the client has already told us the answer: the parts CSV import is real but lossy (no .xlsx, no row errors, no dup report). Solving that well on this stack often removes the Power App ask.

The doc must prove
  • Dataverse is not a free M365 add-on — $20/u/mo + capacity overage is the TCO driver
  • 6 statuses + 6 timestamps + canTransition does not fit Power Fx business rules cleanly
  • Custom connector to Convex keeps one source of truth — mirror is eventual drift
  • Import = client SheetJS parse + Zod row errors + by_code dedup; export = client SheetJS + lazy jspdf-autotable
The doc must not do
  • Propose hosting Convex inside Power Apps / SPFx (impossible)
  • Claim M365 Business Standard includes Power Apps premium/Dataverse (it doesn't)
  • Duplicate Zod schemas in Power Fx — two validations = drift
  • Commit a .msapp or convex/http.ts route (research only)

Risks & traps we explicitly avoid

Trap: “Rebuild is free because we have M365.” Dataverse + premium connectors are metered; $20/u/mo × 20 users × 12 = $4,800/yr before capacity. The doc prices this in a worked example and ties it to a kill rule for the companion PoC.
Trap: Two validations, two truths. Zod schemas live in src/lib/schemas and are shared client/server in this app. A Power Apps rebuild would re-encode them in Power Fx — drift guaranteed. The doc warns against duplicating schemas and recommends Convex-behind-connector if companion is built.
Trap: Import as a second source of truth. Naïve import that writes directly to parts without row errors or dedup silently corrupts catalogue. The design requires preview + per-row Zod + by_code dedup + server re-parse.
