02

Introduction

Purpose

This document defines the functional and non-functional requirements for the Cedric Masters Autos workshop and dealership management system. It is the baseline agreement between product, engineering, and quality — the single reference for what the system does, how it behaves, and how it will be verified.

Scope

# In Scope — MVP

  • After-sales service: customers, check-in, technician intake, parts catalogue, labour rates, invoicing, payments, 8-status job workflow
  • Vehicle sales: inventory, leads/CRM, sales orders, delivery handover
  • Administration: 7 roles, user management, VAT & labour settings
  • Cross-cutting: immutable audit trail, role-based access control, Excel import of parts

# Out of Scope — Post-MVP

  • Customer self-service portal (history, downloads, online booking)
  • Reporting & dashboards (revenue, productivity, parts usage)
  • Multi-branch / multi-tenant organisation scoping
  • Trade-in management & sales commission tracking
  • Accounting integration (QuickBooks, Sage) & Excel export
  • Email/SMS delivery of reset codes & invoices

03

Overall Description

Product Perspective

Cedric Masters Autos is a realtime, serverless web application. The frontend is TanStack Start (React 19) with TanStack Router and Tailwind v4; the backend is Convex (typed, reactive database + functions). Data flows one direction: the browser subscribes to typed queries, and every write is a validated, authorized, audited mutation. Roles are enforced in the backend, never merely hidden in the UI.

User Roles & Operating Environment

Environment & Constraints

  • Modern evergreen browsers; responsive down to mobile
  • Deployment: Vercel (Nitro adapter, .output/), Convex cloud backend
  • Auth: Convex Auth, password provider (email + password)
  • Validation: Zod v4 shared between client and server
  • Money: integer kobo everywhere; VAT rounded to kobo
  • TypeScript 7 (tsgo) — typecheck ~2 s

Assumptions & Dependencies

Assumptions

Staff are named users with assigned roles; one person may hold one role at a time; NGN is the transactional currency in v1.

Dependencies

Convex availability, Google Fonts for rendering, browser print support for job cards and invoices.

Open Questions

Discounts & limits, VAT exemptions, corporate net-30 credit terms, first-time vs returning customer flows.

Functional Requirements

Service Module — After-Sales

FR-SVC-01 · Customer Database

PriorityP0ActorCSR · Manager · Admin
RequirementThe system SHALL maintain a central customer record (name, phone, optional email, address) and SHALL support create, update, and search by name or phone.
VerificationUnit + E2E; manual search smoke test (convex/customers.ts, search indexes on name & phone)

FR-SVC-02 · One-Stop Creation

PriorityP0ActorCSR · Manager · Admin
RequirementThe system SHALL allow a customer, their vehicle, and a job to be created in a single flow without leaving the form.

FR-SVC-03 · Repair History

PriorityP0ActorCSR · Finance · Manager · Admin
RequirementThe system SHALL show the full repair history for a customer (all jobs, vehicles, invoices, and statuses) on the customer detail page.

FR-SVC-04 · Walk-In Check-In

PriorityP0ActorCSR · Manager · Admin
RequirementThe system SHALL provide a 3-step check-in flow: locate/create the customer, locate/create the vehicle, capture the complaint and create the job in checkedIn status with a timestamp.

FR-SVC-05 · Appointment Booking

PriorityP0ActorCSR · Manager · Admin
RequirementThe system SHALL support booking appointments with Today/Week/Month range views, SHALL reject past dates (server + client), and SHALL require vehicle make, model, plate, and complaint. Appointments move scheduled → checkedIn → cancelled.

FR-SVC-06 · Technician Job Intake

PriorityP0ActorTechnician · Manager · Admin
RequirementThe system SHALL allow assignment of a technician, recording of a diagnosis, and transition of the job through the state machine. A technician SHALL only act on jobs assigned to them (admin/manager excepted).

FR-SVC-07 · Spare Parts Catalogue

PriorityP0ActorInventory Manager · Manager · Admin
RequirementThe system SHALL support CRUD on parts (unique code, description, cost & selling price, stock quantity, reorder level), one-click CSV import, a low-stock badge when stockQty ≤ reorderLevel, and an immutable stock-movement audit trail (in / out / adjust).

FR-SVC-08 · Labour Rate Configuration

PriorityP0ActorFinance · Manager · Admin
RequirementThe system SHALL maintain a lookup of job types to fixed prices (e.g., "Oil change = NGN 5,000") independent of technician or vehicle.

FR-SVC-09 · Invoice Generation

PriorityP0ActorFinance · Manager · Admin
RequirementThe system SHALL compute invoices from part + labour line items with a configurable VAT rate (default 7.5%), snapshot line items, support regeneration (re-sync) until paid, and require manager approval before the customer-facing copy is valid. Regeneration resets approval; a paid invoice is immutable.

FR-SVC-10 · Payment Recording

PriorityP0ActorFinance · Manager · Admin
RequirementThe system SHALL record payments by method (cash / transfer / card), support partial payments with running balance, and auto-mark the invoice paid when amountPaid ≥ grandTotal.

FR-SVC-11 · Job Progress (8-Status State Machine)

PriorityP0ActorAll staff per transition
RequirementThe system SHALL model jobs as: checkedIn → assigned → diagnosed → inProgress → readyForPickup → completed → paid, with waitingRelease as a holding state when parts are needed. Each transition SHALL be guarded by canTransition and SHALL record a per-status timestamp.
Verificationtests/unit/job-utils.test.ts — 5 suites, full transition matrix

FR-SVC-12 · Parts Request Workflow

PriorityP0ActorTechnician → Inventory → Finance
RequirementThe system SHALL move a job to waitingRelease when a technician requests parts, SHALL let Inventory approve/reject/dispatch, SHALL auto-decrement stock on dispatch, add part line items to the job, and re-sync the invoice. Reversal SHALL restore stock, remove line items, and re-sync the invoice. Work SHALL NOT start while approved/pending requests remain.

FR-SVC-13 / 14 · Printable Job Card & Invoice

PriorityP0ActorManager · Technician · CSR · Admin / Finance
RequirementThe system SHALL render A4-printable job cards and invoices populated from backend data, hidden on screen and revealed only for print (hidden print:block).
Sales Module

FR-SLS-01 · Vehicle Inventory

PriorityP0
ActorSales Rep · CSR · Manager · Admin
ReqCRUD vehicles (make, model, year, colour, VIN, plate, cost, selling price) with status inStock / reserved / sold / customerOwned, stock quantity, reorder level, and stock adjustment.

FR-SLS-02 · Customer Leads & CRM

PriorityP0
ActorSales Rep · CSR · Manager · Admin
ReqCRUD leads with stages new → contacted → qualified → won / lost, follow-up notes timeline, and next-follow-up reminders.

FR-SLS-03 · Sales Orders

PriorityP0
ActorSales Rep · CSR · Manager · Admin
ReqCreate order (agreed price + deposit), auto-reserve vehicle, track balance & payment log, complete only when balance is zero, cancel restores stock.

FR-SLS-04 · Delivery Handover

PriorityP0

Administration

FR-ADM-01 · User Roles & Management

PriorityP0
ActorAdmin
ReqAdmin SHALL list users, assign any of the 7 roles, activate/deactivate accounts. Deactivated users SHALL fail server-side auth checks. A one-time bootstrap promotes the first admin only when zero admins exist.

FR-ADM-02/03 · Settings

PriorityP0
ActorFinance · Manager · Admin
ReqConfigure the VAT rate (default 7.5%) and manage labour types (name + fixed price).
Cross-Cutting

FR-XCT-01 · Audit Trail

Every mutation SHALL append an immutable audit record: user, action, entity, entity id, timestamp (convex/lib/audit.ts).

FR-XCT-02 · RBAC

Server SHALL default-deny: a null role is never authorized, admin bypasses, every mutation guards its roles via requireRole. UI reflects the same matrix.

FR-XCT-05 · Rate Limiting

Targeted fixed-window limiting on financial and write-heavy mutations is proposed, not yet implemented — see the Throttling Plan.

Security Requirements

Controls In Place

  • Authentication — email + password via Convex Auth; reset-password route
  • Authorization — requireUser / requireRole on every protected function; default-deny
  • Data isolation — technicians scoped to assigned jobs; invoices hidden from technicians
  • Validation — Zod v4 on every write, shared client/server
  • Monetary integrity — integer kobo, no floats
  • Auditability — immutable audit log on all mutations
  • State integrity — transition guards + invoice immutability after payment

Transitions are validated by canTransition in src/lib/job-utils.ts; role gates live in convex/jobs.ts. Each transition stamps its own timestamp field.

W2 · Walk-In Check-In (3-Step)

  1. CSRLocate or create the customerSearch by name or phone; returning customers auto-populate.
  2. CSRLocate or create the vehiclePlate lookup; duplicate plates rejected server-side.
  3. CSRCapture complaint & check inCreates job in checkedIn with checkInTs and audits the action.

W3 · Parts Request → Dispatch

  1. TechnicianRequest parts on an assigned jobStock is validated at request time; the job moves to waitingRelease.
  2. Inventory ManagerReview requestApprove or reject (job returns to diagnosed if rejected).
  3. Inventory ManagerDispatchStock decremented, movements logged, part line items added, invoice re-synced, job resumes.
  4. Inventory ManagerReverse (correction)Stock restored, items removed, invoice re-synced.

W4 · Invoice → Payment

  1. FinanceGenerate / regenerate invoiceSnapshot of part + labour items; VAT applied at configured rate.
  2. ManagerApprove invoiceRequired before payments or markPaid are accepted.
  3. FinanceRecord paymentsCash / transfer / card; partial payments track balance.
  4. FinanceMark paidAuto-flagged when fully settled; invoice becomes immutable.

W5 · Sales Order → Handover

  1. Sales RepCreate order from a leadAgreed price + deposit; vehicle stock decremented and reserved when zero.
  2. Sales RepRecord paymentsBalance tracked; payments capped at remaining balance.
  3. Sales RepComplete orderOnly when balance is zero; vehicle marked sold.
  4. Sales RepDelivery handoverChecklist: keys, manual, toolkit, inspection — recorded against the rep.
